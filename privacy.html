Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026
Data controller: Mohamed Rida Boujbel Ben Ali (hereinafter “Salati” or “the Controller”) · Contact: soporte.salati@gmail.com

In one sentence: SALATI has no servers of its own, requires no sign-up, stores no personal data and sells nothing to anyone. Everything you configure stays on your device.

1. Who is responsible

This policy describes how SALATI works. SALATI is a free application for checking prayer times, the Qibla direction, nearby mosques, the Hijri calendar and the Quran. The application contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

For any question about this policy or about your data, write to soporte.salati@gmail.com.

2. Data we do NOT collect

SALATI has no database and no server of its own. Specifically:

  • There is no sign-up, user account or password.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number or any personal identifier.
  • No advertising profiles are built and no tracking identifier is assigned to you.
  • No data is sold, rented or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or file storage.

3. Location permission (GPS)

Location is optional: you can use the application by searching for your city manually and never grant it. When you do grant it, it is used solely and exclusively for three things:

  • Calculating prayer times accurately for your position.
  • Calculating the Qibla direction, which is computed on your own device and sent nowhere.
  • Finding nearby mosques on the map.

Your coordinates are not stored on any server of ours, because no such server exists. They are saved on your device so we do not have to ask you every time, and they are sent to the third-party services described in section 6, which need them to return the result of the query.

You can revoke the permission at any time in your operating system settings. The application will keep working: it will ask you to pick a city manually.

4. Notifications permission

It is used only to alert you at each prayer time and, if you enable it, to play the adhan. These are local notifications: your own device schedules them from the prayer times already calculated.

There are no push notifications from a server, no promotional messages, and no way for us to send anything to your device. You can turn them off from the application settings or from your operating system.

5. Data stored on your device

The application uses local browser or device storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) to remember your preferences: chosen location, language, light or dark theme, alert settings, the tasbih counter and a copy of the day's prayer times so it works offline.

This information never leaves your device and disappears completely when you uninstall the application or clear the browser data.

6. Third-party services

To work without servers of its own, SALATI queries public external services. In doing so, those services unavoidably receive your IP address and, where indicated, your approximate coordinates. Each one is governed by its own privacy policy:

ServicePurposeWhat it receives
Aladhan Prayer times and Hijri date Coordinates, IP
Nominatim (OSM) City search Search text, IP
Overpass API Nearby mosques Coordinates, IP
OpenStreetMap tiles Map imagery Map area, IP
BigDataCloud Your city name from GPS Coordinates, IP
Quran.com Quran text and translations IP
Umami Anonymous usage statistics IP (not stored), country, device type

No Google Fonts. The application's typefaces are served from our own domain, not from Google's CDN. This means your IP address never reaches Google when you open the application.

7. Usage statistics

SALATI uses Umami, a cookie-free analytics tool. It installs no identifiers on your device, does not track your activity across sessions or across websites, and cannot identify any individual.

Only aggregate data is recorded: number of visits, approximate country, device type and which sections of the application are used. Its purpose is simply to know whether the application is useful. Because it is anonymous and cookie-free, it does not require prior consent.

8. Donations and external links

The “Buy me a coffee” and “Help Gaza” buttons are external links that open Ko-fi and PCRF in your browser. SALATI processes no payments and has no access to your banking details. Once you leave the application, those sites' privacy policies apply.

9. Children

The application is suitable for all audiences and collects no personal data, so it does not knowingly process information about minors.

10. Your rights

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) grants you rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability.

Since we keep no data about you, you exercise those rights directly: by revoking permissions in your operating system and uninstalling the application or clearing its data, at which point all stored information disappears irreversibly. If you still have questions, write to soporte.salati@gmail.com.

11. Security

All communication with external services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. As there are no servers of our own and no user accounts, there are no databases that could be compromised.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date in the header will be updated and the new version will be published at this same address. We recommend reviewing it from time to time.
